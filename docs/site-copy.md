# **Home Page Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What services do you provide**

We coordinate property maintenance, preservation, inspection, and general repair work. Supported trades may include plumbing, electrical, HVAC, painting, carpentry, lawn care, debris removal, winterization, turnover work, and related services.

### **Which areas do you serve**

Our launch coverage includes selected markets in Virginia, Michigan, New York, Texas, and Washington. Service availability depends on the property location and the requested trade. Send us the ZIP code and scope so we can confirm coverage.

### **How do I request an estimate**

Use the Request a Quote form or contact us by phone or email. Include the property address, requested service, photos when available, and any scheduling requirements. We will review the scope and advise on the next step.

### **Do you provide emergency support**

After-hours requests are accepted for urgent property needs. Response time and availability depend on the location, trade, site access, and nature of the issue.

### **How do service professionals get started**

Complete the Become a Service Partner form with your contact information, coverage area, trades, and business credentials. If your services match a current need, our team will contact you about onboarding requirements.

### **Do you use standard pricing**

Pricing depends on the approved scope, property condition, labor, materials, access, timing, and local market. Estimates should separate labor and material costs and identify any assumptions or exclusions.

### **How are service partners paid**

Approved service partners are paid according to the terms in their executed agreement. Payment method, documentation requirements, and timing are confirmed during onboarding.

### **What documents may be required from a service partner**

Requirements depend on the trade and location. Titan Core Builders may request business information, tax documentation, licenses, proof of insurance, coverage details, and a signed service partner agreement before assignments are issued.

# **Design and Development Requirements**

## **Visual Layout**

* Use a clean white or off-white background with deep navy navigation, headings, and primary buttons.  
* Use muted copper only for small accents, icons, links, and hover states.  
* Keep the logo prominent but compact in the header. Do not use a full-screen logo treatment.  
* Use one strong residential maintenance image in the hero and one relevant image for each service category.  
* Use simple line icons for maintenance, preservation, inspection, response, documentation, and coverage.  
* Avoid animations beyond subtle hover and reveal effects. The site should load quickly on mobile connections.  
* Design mobile-first. Buttons and form fields must remain easy to tap and read on small screens.

## **Forms and Email Routing**

* Send client form notifications to info@TitanCoreBuilders.com unless a dedicated operations address is approved.  
* Send service partner form notifications to info@TitanCoreBuilders.com until a dedicated vendor address is created.  
* Use clear subject lines such as New Client Inquiry and New Service Partner Inquiry.  
* Store the form submission securely or send it through the selected form service. Do not expose email credentials in website code.  
* Add spam protection, server-side validation, rate limiting, and a hidden bot field or comparable anti-spam control.  
* Do not allow file uploads at launch. Request licenses, insurance certificates, W-9 forms, and property photos through an approved secure process after initial contact.  
* Send a simple confirmation email to the person who submitted the form.

## **Accessibility and Performance**

* Meet WCAG 2.2 AA color contrast for text, buttons, links, form labels, and focus states.  
* Provide descriptive alternative text for meaningful images and empty alternative text for decorative images.  
* Allow full keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators.  
* Use persistent form labels and plain-language validation messages.  
* Compress images and serve modern formats where supported.  
* Use HTTPS, a valid security certificate, and automated backups.

## **Search and Analytics**

* Use the approved page titles and meta descriptions in this document.  
* Add Organization and LocalBusiness structured data only after the final public address, phone number, hours, and service area are approved.  
* Connect Google Search Console and an approved privacy-conscious analytics tool.  
* Create and submit an XML sitemap. Block duplicate staging pages from indexing.

# **Legal Page Requirements**

## **Privacy Policy**

The Privacy Policy should identify Titan Core Builders LLC, the effective date, and the approved business contact details. It should explain the information collected through client and service partner forms, how the company uses that information, service providers that may process it, retention, security, cookies or analytics, user choices, and the method for privacy requests.

Because the partner form may collect business credentials and insurance status, the final policy should be reviewed by qualified U.S. counsel before publication.

## **Terms of Use**

The Terms of Use should explain that website information is general, a submitted form does not create a service agreement, estimates and schedules require written confirmation, services may be performed through approved subcontractors or service partners, and availability depends on location and scope. The final terms should also address intellectual property, prohibited use, external links, disclaimers, limitation of liability, governing law, updates, and contact information.

Client contracts, work orders, service partner agreements, payment terms, insurance obligations, indemnity, and trade-specific requirements should remain in separate signed agreements. They should not be replaced by website terms.
